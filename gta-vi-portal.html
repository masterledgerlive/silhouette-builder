🎮 WHY THIS DOOR, WHY NOW

On November 19 the whole world loads into a fictional Florida. We are building the real one. Pinellas County XD is a playable layer over the actual county we live in — Clearwater, Largo, Dunedin, St. Pete — built from real city data, where the community doesn't just watch the city: it votes on it, funds it, builds it, and test-drives every change in the simulation before a single real shovel moves.

🗺️ THE PINELLAS XD LAYER

A meta-city: the real streets, the real infrastructure under them, and a game on top. Fill a pothole. Raise a slow-down sign. Re-route a road. In XD you propose it, the community votes it, the people fund it, and the simulation proves it — then the real city hall conversation starts with a working model in hand instead of an opinion.
