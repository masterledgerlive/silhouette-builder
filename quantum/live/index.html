Design consequence (locked in advance)

Garnet's raw parallel lanes must never seed separate security receipts. The Genesis Gate design switches to single-lane sequential draws through a randomness extractor. The hopeful assumption was abandoned in the open, in the desk's own log — that is what the falsifier is for.

Learn the pieces

Why a Bell test first?

CHSH is the cheapest question whose answer cannot be faked classically. If a machine cannot beat S = 2.0 here, nothing built on it can be trusted. Garnet answered 2.664.

Why lock the bands before running?

Because a band drawn after the result is just a story. Every case in this program publishes its pass/fail lines before the machine is touched — and publishes the counts either way.

Why publish a KILL?

A hidden failure teaches nothing and corrodes everything. The counts, the job ID, and the consequence are all on this page. Anyone can re-run the analyzer on the archived counts and must reach the same verdict.
