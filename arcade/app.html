⚠ You’re in a preview window

This page opened inside another app’s preview sheet. That kind of window blocks cameras and microphones by rule — no game or room can reach them from in here, and it isn’t the game’s fault.

First step: get this page into Safari. Tap the compass icon at the bottom of this sheet (or the Share button → “Open in Safari”). Then come back to this page there and follow the steps below.

Put the Arcade on your home screen

You’re in a browser — good, this window can install the app. Three taps:

  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll the list and tap “Add to Home Screen.”
  3. Tap Add in the top corner.

Then close this tab and open the Arcade from the new gold-wave icon on your home screen. Inside it, your camera and mic work like they should — faces over avatars, voices in the room.

On other phones/browsers the same door is usually in the ⋮ menu: “Add to Home screen” or “Install app.”
