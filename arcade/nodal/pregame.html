NODE — the first blob token. Issued only against sealed prediction scores: 1 NODE per point earned on a sealed card or in the point game. No premine, no sale, no admin mint — the book is the mint. A NODE is a receipt of verified foresight: every unit names the match, the card, and the points it came from. Balances are derived, never stored as truth — anyone can recompute them from the chain and get these same numbers.

A predicted fault is worth more than a predicted winner. A predicted ace is worth five winners. The finer your foresight, the more it pays — that's the game.

Turned in before first ball, the card's hash seals; after that it cannot change — it can only be right or wrong, point by point, in public.
The lanes are the first entrants; the format is open to any person or AI (POLYMATH). Every score on this page traces to a sealed record — the chain verifies end to end, and verify_loop re-checks it every 15 minutes.
