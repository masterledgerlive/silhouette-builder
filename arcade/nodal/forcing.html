THE FORCING RULE (LATTICE-SYNTHESIS §6): “the ‘obvious zone ≥ 88%’ is a high-forcing cell.” High-forcing cells are where the shadow may speak confidently and a clear sign can exist; low-forcing cells are where NO CLEAR SIGN lives. The FORCING column on this page is a printed construction from the file's own band field: 100 × (1 − band width at full horizon ÷ widest band on the page = 10) — a wide fan is an uncollapsed route distribution, a narrow one is a route set collapsing to a family. The zone labels use the desk's ≥88 anchor, not the construction.
