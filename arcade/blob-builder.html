📚 MY COMPOSITIONS

HOW THE MATCH WORKS: every thing on the shelf says what it DOES and what it NEEDS. Every world says what it ACCEPTS and what it HAS (graves, hearts, a sky…). The builder checks the match before a drop lands — if a need is missing, the drop is refused, kindly and out loud, in the yellow caption bar. Nothing here is pretend: the worlds, characters, layers and mechanics are real blobs on the Walrus network, and a sealed composition is a real record with a real hash. Demo worlds are registered names whose blobs are not poured yet — they sit on the shelf so nobody forgets them, and they refuse drops until they're packaged.
