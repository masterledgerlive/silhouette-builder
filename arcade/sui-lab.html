⚡ LATENCY LAB — CUT THE 30-SECOND LAG

The Speed Run's working model. Our measured ~30s glass-to-glass lag, stacked by stage — then flip the playbook toggles (Series 8, P3) and watch the total fall. A model, not a measurement — stage values are modeled from the research: Walrus arXiv paper (write latency <25s small blobs, ~6s fixed metadata+PoA overhead), Sui Mysticeti (~390–500ms consensus, ~250ms owned-object fast path), and classic HLS physics (6s segments × 3-segment buffer = 18–30s).

30.0s GLASS-TO-GLASS (BROADCAST TIER)
INTERACTIVE TIER (WebRTC): ~0.5s glass-to-glass — sub-second, for gaming & watch parties. Scales per room, not per CDN. The broadcast tier above and the Walrus archive keep running underneath it.

PHYSICS FLOOR: you can't stream a chunk before it exists, and you can't beat light. Broadcast floor ≈ chunk + light + certification (~2s in practice). Interactive floor ≈ light + encode (<0.5s). "Nothing" = nothing you can feel.

🧠 BRAIN RECURSIVE MEMORY · V0.1 LIVE

The lab is growing its own brain — the same recursive memory the keeper will run. Every memory is a content-addressed blob (sha256 is the ID — any consumer can verify it), with lineage (what it derived from), invalidation, never deletion (corrections supersede; history is never rewritten), and sleep-time consolidation (the brain links related memories while it rests). Three tiers: History (append-only log) · Memory (versioned blobs) · Scratchpad (per-task). Recall returns memories with proofs, not just text.
