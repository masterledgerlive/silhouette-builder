🧊🐰 BUNNY CUBE — the meadow, alive

A whole new Bunny game, and a different species from the 2D cabinet (which stays frozen as a first attempt, per the versioning law). This is a 3D open world: the film's own 2008 production models — Bunny, Frank, Rinky, Gamera, the apple tree, even the meadow terrain — converted once from the original .blend artifacts into game objects, blobbed to Walrus, sha256-verified in your browser, and assembled into the cube. You enter through THE DOOR, run around as Bunny, pick up apples and throw them. Sandbox play — no scores, just the meadow.

🌳 WHAT YOU'RE STANDING IN

Every object in this world is a Walrus blob with a printed record — name (blob id), key, proof (sha256), measure (bytes). The cast and the set were already rendered in 2008 for the film Big Buck Bunny (Blender Foundation, CC-BY 3.0): this desk's one conversion pass — Blender, headless, at the artifact→object boundary — baked the rigs at game weight (subsurf 1, widgets and cages excluded) into glTF objects. The game was then injected into the cube: one manifest names every object, and this page proves each one before it appears. The 12 apples hanging in the tree sit at the exact points the 2008 layout artists marked with empties in the tree file.
