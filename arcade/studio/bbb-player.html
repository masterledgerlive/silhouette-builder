HOW THIS PLAY IS PROVEN

The simulation is deterministic: a seeded heart, no wall clock, no trust. A run is recorded as a tape (your steering, one mark per frame) plus a state chain (hashes of the sim at fixed marks). Anyone can cold-replay the tape from the blobs and must land on the same chain — that is the whole proof, and it is why a high score here carries its replay. In PROOF mode the page re-runs the machine's genesis run and compares chains.

🏆 THE SCORE CHAIN — every score carries its replay
