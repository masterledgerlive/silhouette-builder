HOW TO PLAY

🏃 Move with the thumb pad (left) or WASD/arrows. Drag to look.
☀️ Day (2 min): scavenge. Zombies shamble but none spawn.
🌙 Night (2 min): the graves give up the dead — 2 × the night number, two by two. What you don't kill carries over. The horde compounds until it's impossible. Your score: nights survived + kills.
🍎 Red apple: +1 heart. ✨ Golden apple: appears briefly, once a night — grab it and every zombie dies. 🟢 Green apple: full health + 10 s invincible — your touch kills.
🧰 The chest holds one weapon at a time: 🔫 rifle (pinpoint line), 🚀 rocket (blast zone), 🧨 dynamite (3-2-1 ticker, biggest zone, no safe sides). FIRE button shoots.
🏠 The two-story house is real cover — up the stairs, hold the floor. The graves are by the dead tree.
🧟 If one reaches you: flash, scratch, blood — −1 heart. Five hearts. Zero = eaten.
👥 Two players: one hosts, one joins — as a fellow survivor, or as a zombie: die, watch-and-wait, and the graveyard issues you a new body. Kill all the good guys and the zombie board takes your time.
