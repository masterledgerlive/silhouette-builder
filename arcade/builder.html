LEVEL 0 · THE LAW ⚖

Every blob game in this arcade obeys one law, stored in one blob that every machine shares:

· Fixed tick, integer math — no floating point, so every device replays identically.
· Seeded but true to the field — the seed deals controls, opponents, and world events from hidden streams; parameters stay inside the field's real bounds.
· The tape is the truth — scores are derived by replaying seed + input tape, chained by SHA-256. Never trusted, always re-computed.
· Wallet is the who — run key = sha256(wallet | seed | nonce).
· Learn before play · spin-up gate — no level, game, or builder advances until the previous one is proven by a full blob spin-up.

LEVEL 4 · SPIN-UP 🚀 what makes a forged object a living game

  
Your object is a design — remembered, hashable, fetchable. The stones show the rest of the road, and every step is one this walkthrough has already proven in front of you:

  
1 · Engine — a deterministic simulator implementing your law (the stones' engines are blobs in the registry).

  2 · Tests — honest verifies, inflation rejected, canned tape dies on a fresh seed.

  3 · Blobs — manifest + law + engine PUT to Walrus, read back, hash-matched.

  4 · Cabinet — an HTML page that boots from the blobs, gates play behind the lesson, records tapes.

  5 · Chain — a genesis blob for your machine; every proven score reblobs a new head.

  6 · Hall — a verifier that walks your chain and replays every run for anyone, any time.

  
That full engine build is the next level of this walkthrough — it unlocks the same way everything here unlocks: as each new stone is mastered and its objects join the registry. Until then: your object is on the chain, the cabinets are open, and the Hall is watching. Go play a blob gameplay and be remembered.
