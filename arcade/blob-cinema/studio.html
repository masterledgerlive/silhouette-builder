2. HOW IT WORKS (hover any dotted word for details)

✂️ CUT

We slice your video into ~1MB pieces. Like cutting a film reel into segments. We cut only between video fragments, never in the middle of one.

🔒 LOCK

Each piece gets locked with its own secret key. We use encryption so nobody can watch without the key. Each key is made from the previous key — a key chain.

🌐 SCATTER

We upload each locked piece to the Walrus network. Walrus is decentralized storage — many computers hold pieces, no single owner. Each upload returns a blob ID (proof of location).

🔗 LINK

Each piece remembers where the previous piece lives. We upload forward (first piece first) so you can start watching immediately. The starting key is all anyone needs to follow along.

▶️ PLAY

The player needs only the first piece's location + key. It fetches piece 1, unlocks it, checks its fingerprint, plays it, then follows the pointer to piece 2. While you watch, it's already fetching ahead.

🔑 Share key: one key to share, generated as soon as piece 1 is up — even while the rest uploads.

♾️ KEEP

Every piece carries an expiry date. One hour before a piece dies, the keeper makes a fresh locked copy (same key, new nonce → new blob ID) and points the film at it. Turn auto-renew OFF and the keeper stands down — the pieces die on schedule, permanently.
