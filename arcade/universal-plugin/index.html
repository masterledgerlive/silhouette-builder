✂️ Storage Partition Agreement — signup

Joining partitions your storage into two slices. The Commons Slice carries everyone's shards and the public commons (food data first) — it is the only slice that earns. The Sovereign Slice is yours alone: encrypted, priced in storage units that never float, read by nobody without your key. To earn, at least 20% must be Commons (a working floor, to be pilot-tested). The floor is a wage rule, never a caste line: below it you are a full member who simply earns nothing.
