THE SHAPE OF IT

A game here is not a website with a database. It is a bundle of blobs — law, manifest, engine, mechanic objects — plus a score chain of blobs, plus a thin HTML cabinet that boots from the blobs and a Hall that verifies everything by replay. The chain remembers; the devices compute; the checking makes it true.

⚖ ENGINE LAW
one law blob, shared by every stone — fixed tick, integer math, seeded but true, tape is truth, wallet is who

🏆 HALL
the side verifier: walks the chain to genesis, replays every run, renders proof-of-play on demand

🔑 THE MASTER RENDER KEY — DOUBLE SWITCHED

What you see is a render. What decides the game is a deal — and the deal is never in the render. Two switches stand between a player and the parameters that trigger outcomes:

RUN KEY (switch 1 — identity)     sha256( wallet | seed | nonce )
  binds the run to its wallet. Nobody else's key verifies this tape.

DEAL (switch 2 — the hidden parameters)   seed → separate PRNG streams
  stream A deals the CONTROLS (slap remap)     stream B deals the OPPONENT (speed/lag/err)
  stream C deals the WORLD (stimulus ticks / serves, one by one, as needed)

RENDER shows only: the field, the flash, the ball — never the streams.
So nobody — player, bot, or page insider — knows the correct play or the
parameter set in advance. Correct play cannot be precomputed; it can only
be PLAYED, taped, and then PROVEN by replay against the same deal.

That is why the Predictor AI scores like it does: it plays the render. The tape, judged against the hidden deal, is the only truth — and replay is the judge that cannot be bribed, because it re-derives the deal from the seed and the score from the tape.

🚀 SPIN-UP — the blob plays itself

Any machine can spin a game up from the blobs alone, with its player enlocked to the HTML cabinet and the blockchain record:

1 · Fetch the manifest blob → sha-verify (the cabinet does this on boot, live).
2 · Fetch the engine blob → the exact simulator bytes; the browser twin must match it bit-for-bit (cross-tested 16/16 on Stone 2).
3 · The cabinet plays: seed dealt, tape recorded, score derived by replay — live play and independent replay must agree before submit is even offered.
4 · The score is reblobbed as a new chain head; the old head becomes its prev. Nothing is overwritten; history only grows.
5 · The Hall walks head → genesis and replays every run. A spin-up is proven when a stranger's machine can do steps 1–5 and get the same numbers.
