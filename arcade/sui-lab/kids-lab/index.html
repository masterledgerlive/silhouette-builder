For grown-ups: this prototype is the Kids Quantum Lab designed 2026-10-23 — a teaching front-end on the house quantum system. Everything runs on a local emulator (a program that imitates a quantum computer) at zero cost: the cost line on every receipt reads 0 GENIQ, the house token's free-route rule. No accounts, no payments, no data collected or sent anywhere, no real quantum hardware — those doors exist in the design and stay shut until a parent and David open them, in that order. A child's result is shared publicly only with a parent's explicit approval. Puzzle physics is real and unit-tested: coin-flip statistics, Bell-pair correlations, one-round Grover search (exact for 4 doors), and the CHSH game (classical limit 75%, quantum team ≈ 85%).
