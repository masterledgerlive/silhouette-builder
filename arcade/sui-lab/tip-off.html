3 · THE REAL MATH OF USAGE — HQC

Quantum time on Quantinuum hardware is metered in HQCs (Helios Quantum Credits), by a published formula — no mystery pricing:

HQC = 5 + C × (N1q + 10·N2q + 5·Nm) / 5000
C = shots · N1q = one-qubit gates · N2q = two-qubit gates · Nm = measurements

✏️ The documented worked example

A circuit with 8 one-qubit gates, 1 ZZPhase (two-qubit) gate and 3 measurements, run for 100 shots:
5 + 100 × (8 + 10 + 15) / 5000 = 5 + 0.66 = 5.66 HQC — exact, from Quantinuum's own documentation. In our gateway's pricing rule (disclosed margin ×1.2, rounded up), that job quotes at 7 GENIQ.

🎛️ Our gateway, measured live

On the live TEST GENIQ gateway: a 500-round CHSH match quotes at 9 TEST GENIQ — ceil((5 + 500×22/5000) × 1.2) — locks in escrow, settles, burns 9, and the ledger's conservation check still balances to the credit. 🧾 The design in one paragraph

GENIQ exists only as numbers inside one shared ledger object — no coin is ever minted, so there is nothing to trade, pump, or rug. Quotes bind to a SHA-256 commitment of the exact job; escrow locks before execution; settlement verifies the price rule in Move (actual × 5 ≤ quoted × 6) and burns the charge on delivery; if actual cost ever beat 1.2× the quote, the job aborts and the escrow releases in full. Receipts are soulbound and hash-stamped, so results enter a learning corpus (Yaae's Watch verifies every hash before learning from it) and peer-review podcasts trigger off real, recomputable work.
