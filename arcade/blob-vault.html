🗝️ The promise: your real secret is never written down anywhere — not on the chain, not in the blobs. The chain keeps only name tags and a scrambled key. The blobs keep only confetti. One golden key unscrambles everything… and if you melt the golden key, the secret is gone forever — even though every piece is still sitting out there in the open.

📖 THE STORY — HOW IT WORKS, STEP BY STEP, LIKE A CARTOON

📝

STEP 1 · THE SECRET

You start with something precious: an API key, a password, a secret code. Right now it is written in plain letters — anyone who sees it, owns it.

WHY IT MATTERS: a plain secret is a loaded secret. We never let it travel plain.

🎲

STEP 2 · THE SCRAMBLE MACHINE

The machine invents a brand-new random key — the Vault Key — used for this batch and nothing else. It scrambles your secret into confetti (real AES-256 encryption).

WHY IT WORKS: confetti can't be read backwards. Without the Vault Key, the scramble is just noise, forever.

🔒

STEP 3 · THE SECOND LOCK

Now the One Key — born from your master password — scrambles the Vault Key itself. Double locked: the secret is locked by the Vault Key, and the Vault Key is locked by the One Key.

WHY IT WORKS: stealing the scrambled key gets you a locked box. Stealing the confetti gets you confetti. You need both locks opened, in order.

✂️

STEP 4 · THE SPLIT

The confetti is chopped into 5 puzzle pieces that only make sense all together. Any 4 pieces = still confetti.

WHY IT WORKS: no single house, hacker, or hard drive ever holds enough to rebuild anything.

🌍

STEP 5 · THE SCATTER

Each piece flies to a different blob house, far from the others — blob storage, dispersed by design. Every piece gets a name tag (its blob ID, the fingerprint of the piece itself).

WHY IT WORKS: the pieces are spread out like stars. Losing one house loses nothing readable; finding one house finds nothing readable.

⛓️

STEP 6 · THE CHAIN NOTEBOOK

The chain writes down ONLY: the name tags, the scrambled Vault Key, and the batch number. Never the real secret. Never the One Key. Never the un-scrambled anything.

WHY IT WORKS: the whole world can read the notebook all day and learn nothing. It is a map to confetti, signed by a locked box.

🗝️

STEP 7 · THE WAY BACK

Want your secret back? Your One Key unscrambles the Vault Key → the Vault Key unscrambles the confetti → the pieces, gathered by their name tags, click back together. The real secret appears — only for you, only then.

WHY IT WORKS: every lock opens in reverse order, and only the One Key starts the chain.

🔁

STEP 8 · THE REBLOB

Adding more secrets later? The whole batch goes back through the machine: new Vault Key, new confetti, new pieces, new name tags. The old pieces are struck DEAD in the notebook.

WHY IT WORKS: the batch keeps getting remixed, so yesterday's scatter is never today's map. Old tags point at pieces that no longer matter.

🔥

STEP 9 · THE MELT

Done with a secret forever? Melt the scrambled Vault Key. The pieces stay scattered, the notebook stays public — and none of it can ever be unscrambled again. Not by us. Not by anyone.

WHY IT WORKS: destruction is one small delete, not a hunt through a hundred houses. Kill the key, kill the secret — the confetti takes care of itself.

🤖 WHY AGENTIC AI LOVES THIS — AGENTS CARRY CONFETTI, HUMANS HOLD THE KEY

An AI agent can be handed the notebook — the name tags and the scrambled key — and can fetch pieces, carry them, count them, and rebuild the confetti… and still know nothing. The agent only gets a working secret at the moment the One Key holder unwraps it, for exactly the job at hand. Agents become couriers of locked boxes instead of walking lists of passwords — and a courier can be fired by melting one key.
