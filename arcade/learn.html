How this school works. Every podcast series here is a class, taught like a master class: a credited university source for every fact (institution → author → course, paper or dissertation → year → free or paid), a peer-review panel in every lesson, and roots under every class — the free courses, papers, data and findings the lessons grow from, so anyone can go deeper than the episode. Free sources first, always. Paid sources are named and parked, never hidden. On October 23, 2026, David gave the word: everything live. Every produced lesson below is published and playable; lessons still in growth are marked as they grow.
