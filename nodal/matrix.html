THE 1-2-3 MATRIX
One chained matrix, not three sheets: Set 1 exact score → every Set 2 fork off that result → every decider fork off that path, a probability at every fork. Forks are CENSUS — counted from 53,530 completed best-of-3 matches (Sackmann ATP+WTA 2015–2026, re-seated to the Set-1 winner; the recomputation reproduces the published COMBO-KEY slope exactly). Where a live continuation needs the point-by-point engine it is COMPUTED — the desk DP — and labeled, never dressed as census. Cells counted on fewer than 30 matches are tagged THIN with their n.
