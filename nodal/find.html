What FIND cannot see (printed, not hidden)

Our wire is the ESPN tour feed: ATP/WTA tour events. Challenger and ITF events do not ride it — in season or out — so a Challenger match will never appear here live; FIND says so out loud and points at the official listing and the players' archive records instead. External listings are find-sources only and never feed a call. Records save client-side as certified downloads (SHA-256, source + arrival clock, schema v2); they enter the sealed chain when the desk imports them, and append to the player's sealed additions there.
