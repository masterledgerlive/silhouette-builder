⚖️ THE TWO SCORES — NEVER BLENDED

PREDICTION SCORE — the frozen pick's own track: right or wrong, and how honest its printed probability was (Brier calibration). Picks are sealed before first ball and never edited after. The number printed on a pick is its simulated probability of being right — the engines' model probability, not a promise; its complement is printed as the RISK a follower takes.

LIVE PROFIT SCORE — the live play is scored on PROFIT, not on who won the match: it does not matter who wins; what matters is what was profited. A specialist can back the match loser and still win this board if the moves profited — and backing the winner while losing money scores as a loss. Verdicts: WON WITH PROFIT / CLOSED AT A LOSS, with the count of profit-making moves vs wrong moves.
