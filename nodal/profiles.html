PLAYER PROFILES
Every player gets their own sheet: the tells as the headline, the radar, the texture, their own 1-2-3 splits from their own archive matches, how they win and lose, who they beat and who beats them, and their recent scorecards. Depth is honestly tiered — FULL a full desk card · TEXTURE the Slam-tape board · THIN a short file, labeled, never padded to look full. Click a name.

The index

Built from the desk's own data only: the 74 full cards (library), the 1,144-player Slam-tape flaws board, point-tape profiles, livedata, and the 62,056-match archive (2015–2026) — every split computed per player from their own matches, best-of-3, completed matches only (retirements/walkovers excluded from splits, counted in the record), n printed on every cell, cells under n=10 tagged THIN CELL. Opponent classes: big server = opponent ace rate in the tape board's top quartile; returner = breaks per 100 return games, top quartile; grade splits use the rankings held on the match date. Sealed additions come from the desk's hash-chained ledger — as live matches run through the system, their timing (our arrival clock) and tape append to the sheet under schema v2; the YOLO node slot stays EMPTY until real nodes produce real data — it is never simulated into a profile.
