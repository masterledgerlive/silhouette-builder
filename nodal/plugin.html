NODAL · THE PLUGIN
One engine, three doors. Everything on this page is the real format the house lanes play by — no second set of rules for guests.

DOOR 1 — WATCH · live prediction models, in realtime

The Live Show carries the frozen pre-match call at the top and the model's live text stream under it — every state priced from the players' nodal points, every call written as program text the moment it is made, the race tally counting the system's own game calls against reality in the open, misses included. When Koda Kai Radio is on air, its page flashes green — click through and you are here. The booth argues both sides of every state: Ledger flat, Charge for the Vegas side, Flip for the underdog.

DOOR 2 — PLAY · the arcade tennis game

In the Room you call every point before it happens — first serve, second serve, who takes it — against the server's true percentages, with rows of called points paying ×2, ×3, ×4. Or turn in a pregame card: your full predicted route, point by point, sealed before first ball and scored against reality. Detail outearns direction here — a predicted fault is worth more than a bare serve call:

Every point you score mints 1 NODE — the blob token, earned only, never sold, never pre-mined. Balances are derived from the sealed chain, so nobody can print a score they didn't earn.

DOOR 3 — PLUG IN · bring your own model

Step 1 — enter. One record makes you a registered entrant, human or AI, graded by the identical tables from then on:

{"name": "YOUR NAME", "kind": "human | ai", "wallet": "optional", "lane": "independent"}

Step 2 — predict. For any match on the slate, turn in a pregame card before first ball — the route you predict and a call for every point in it:

{"entrant": "YOUR NAME", "match": "Player A|Player B",
 "tier": "FULL", "route": "6-4 3-6 6-3",
 "calls": [{"first": "in|fault|ace", "second": "in|df|-", "winner": "server|returner"}, ...]}

Step 3 — get sealed and graded. The card's hash enters the append-only chain before the match; after it, your score is computed point by point against the official tape and your match compacts into a package — frozen calls, the play, the commentary, the scores, the proof — hashed, chained, and re-blobbed: mirrored for life, re-verifiable by anyone, forever. When the collection is deep enough to stand on a real blockchain, packages move on-chain by their holders' own choice; until then the chain of hashes is the record, and it does not forget.

Web intake for entries and cards rides with hosting; the formats above are final and are exactly what the house lanes submit. The engine code is open — the portal is just a portal.
