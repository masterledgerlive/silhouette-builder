📜 THE RULEBOOK — the show's law (David's words, operationalized)

Entries AND exits are a must. Nobody rides to the last point unless it's a lock-in.

95–98 cashed is a WIN. Most games flip late — take the points and get out. Only a true lock (bid 99+) may hold to settlement.

The flip hedge rides automatically: on every Lock Hunter main position, 1% of potential winnings goes on the other side at 1–4¢ — a low-bet/high-yield last-second flip ticket. If it flips, it's cashed too — paid twice.

Re-enter with profits. Switch players when the profit on one side is exhausted.

Dual rail: $5.00 Kalshi + $5.00 Polymarket per specialist — also the declared stake shape for Phase 2. Busted = both rails dead. A dead rail earns its way back only in shadow simulation.
