WHAT'S INSIDE — FIVE PIECES

👁 THE WATCHER

Reads the BTC numbers right off the live event page — the price to beat, the clock, and the Up / Down percentages — and keeps a clean, timestamped log of every sample. Read-only: it looks, it never touches.

🔘 THE BOARD

Five light-up buttons that float over the trade panel — BUY UP, BUY DOWN, BUY, SELL, REVERSAL. They glow at 55% (get ready) and flash at 62% (the call zone). In locked mode your taps pass straight through to the page underneath. And its Scorekeeper grades your taps: points for sharp presses, slow for late ones, missed exits, and ⚑ flags for greed and gambling — tapping with no light up.

🏋 THE TRIGGER — PRACTICE DECK

A coach, not a trader. Set a buy mark and a sell mark; when the live price crosses one, the coach walks the play in lit steps and clocks your reaction time — so you train your timing before a single real dollar moves. It never trades. Ever.

🔍 THE LENS

Your calls, pinned on the live price line. Every call lands as a marker on the chart with its time and its accuracy %, entries and exits marked, missed first exits flagged — so after the window closes you can see exactly what you did, and learn from it.

🔌 THE VENUE PLUG

Plug in your own Polymarket US or Kalshi API keys — read-only — and the kit proves the connection by reading your balance back to you, then cross-checks the page's BTC numbers against the venue's own every ~15 seconds. The BTC TRUTH line prints MATCH ✓ or ⚠ DIVERGES. Because the BTC data does not lie — and if two honest reads of it ever disagree, you want to know.

🧩 EVERYTHING MOVES

Every panel and button drags anywhere and resizes — and remembers where you put it. Nothing sits in your way. Set the desk up the way you see it.

INSTALL IN 3 STEPS

  1. Unzip the download. You get one folder: blob-buttons-open-kit-v1.3.0.
  2. Open edge://extensions (Edge) or chrome://extensions (Chrome) and turn Developer mode ON.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick that folder. Done.

Then open a live 5-minute BTC Up or Down event on polymarket.us — the Watcher starts reading, the Board lights, the Lens draws the line. That's the whole setup.

YOUR OWN KEYS — THE VENUE PLUG

Optional, and yours alone. Your keys live only in your browser, on your machine, sent only to your own venue as signing headers on reads. The plug READS ONLY — it cannot place orders, by construction. There is no order code in the file at all; you can grep it and check. One click on DISCONNECT wipes the keys.

Polymarket US: sign in at polymarket.us and open the Developer page (polymarket.us/developer). Create an API key — the site shows you a Key ID and a Secret Key. The secret is shown once: copy it somewhere safe right then. Paste both into the plug, press CONNECT, and it reads your balance back to you.

Kalshi: sign in at kalshi.com and open Settings → API Keys. Create a key — Kalshi has you generate a private key file (.key) and gives you a Key ID. Kalshi does not keep a copy of the file, so keep it safe. Paste the Key ID and the contents of the .key file into the plug, press CONNECT. (Kalshi counts money in cents; the plug shows you dollars.)

Honest line #1: Kalshi has no 5-minute BTC game — its fastest is the 15-minute market. The Kalshi plug mirrors that 15-minute data and your account. Nothing more is claimed.
Honest line #2: Polymarket's 5-minute game data lives on the page — its API mirrors your account, not the 5-minute market. That is exactly why the Watcher exists: it reads the game where the game actually is.
