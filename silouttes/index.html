SILHOUETTES — Creator Studio

Design layered shadow-box art and trading cards, pull real pieces from the library, and send them to a laser by two roads. The studio is free and open (MIT). Built for sports and trading card artists — and for the Carbon Cards, whose layers are designed here and etched by laser.

What this place is: a plugin portal to the xTool interface on Atomm. Atomm's AI does this work behind a credit meter; this creator does the same work itself — its own identification lane, its own tracer, its own layer design — so you get the same product as Atomm's AI without paying for the credits. The plugins are the bridge; the laser routes below are the road out.

The two routes. Route A (manual) — export your layers, download, open xTool Creative Space yourself, set the material, make it. It has always worked; it is untouched. Route B (instant-to-xTool) — one tap packages the piece as a ready job: layers in order, material preset attached, staging steps written. It opens in Creative Space with nothing to re-set-up. Honest label, in every package: there is no web-to-laser push; “instant” means zero re-setup, and the final Make press stays with the human standing at the machine — laser work is attended work.

Carbon Cards are designed here

A carbon player card hides its payload across reading channels, so no single way of looking sees the whole thing. The studio lays the layers; the laser etches the original. Channels, graded as the design addendum grades them:

ChannelHow it readsGrade
Prism / foilAngle-split images in refractile foilREAL, small capacity
Anaglyph / 3DLeft/right eye lanes (or lenticular)REAL
RGB channel splitEach color channel carries its own payloadREAL, needs error correction
Pixel / art-masked codeA QR-like code drawn as the artworkREAL, robustness is the work
Nodal pointsPoint positions ARE the dataREAL, quietest channel
UV layerInvisible in daylight, read under UVREAL, security-print standard
Depth / LiDAREmbossed height map decodesPLAUSIBLE — paper proof first
Chip (NTAG 424 DNA)Signed state + tamper flag that breaks with the cardREAL — secret never rides whole on one card

Layers compose with Reed-Solomon thinking: lose one channel and the card degrades instead of dying. The printed header on each card declares which layers its creator used, and in what order to read them.
