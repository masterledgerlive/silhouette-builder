# 🏁 VPN SHOWDOWN — Research for Koda Kai TV
**Researched: 2026-10-20** (all prices "seen on" this date — VPN pricing rotates constantly; nothing here is a promise a price will hold). Research only: nothing signed up for, nothing bought, nobody contacted.

**Our use case, weighted:** family TV in Clearwater FL · country-hopping for free-to-air channels in our own TV library + David's own subscriptions · a shareable affiliate code/link for the TV page · a second provider that still works when the first goes down.

**How the final four were picked:** Gizmodo's October 2026 streaming test (14 services, hands-on, Ookla speed runs) puts NordVPN and ExpressVPN at 14/14 unblocked, Surfshark 13/14, CyberGhost 11/14 — those four are the streaming final four. **Near-miss, honestly noted:** Proton VPN is excellent on privacy and speed but unblocked only 10/14 in the same test, so it misses a *streaming* final. PIA (8/14) is out on unblocking.

> ⚠️ Counting warning: "countries" and "servers" numbers differ between sources because vendors count differently (app-counted locations vs physical countries, marketing totals that change monthly). Figures below name their source; treat all of them as approximate, and re-check in the app during hands-on tests.

## 1. NordVPN
1. **Why it's in the four:** Best unblocking record in the field — **14 of 14** streaming services in Gizmodo's Oct 2026 hands-on test, including Netflix US, BBC iPlayer, Hulu and Peacock, with no extra setup (SmartPlay is built into the apps; SmartDNS included for TVs without VPN apps).
2. **Speed evidence:** Fastest in Gizmodo's Oct 2026 Ookla testing (190 Mbps European base connection, UK/US/Japan servers, 3× daily × 3 days): **7.8% average download loss, 5.5% upload loss** — and only **3.0% loss on a Fire TV Stick**, the smallest stick drop recorded. Cybernews' 2026 average-loss table also ranks it #1 (16% by their different methodology — direction agrees, exact % depends on method). Protocol: **NordLynx** (WireGuard-based, with a privacy layer).
3. **Countries:** **152 countries counted in the app** (Gizmodo, Oct 2026); server totals quoted 7,200–9,300+ across sources/dates. Widest country menu of the four — matters most for country-hopping.
4. **Devices + router:** **10 simultaneous** connections. Router install supported and counts as **one** slot while covering everything behind the router. Native apps for Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV; SmartDNS for the rest.
5. **Best offer seen 2026-10-20:** Basic **$3.49/mo — $94.23 for the first 27 months** (2-year plan + 3 months free, "69% off," Gizmodo deal coverage). Renews at **$139.08/year (~$11.59/mo)** — the renewal jump is real. 30-day money-back guarantee; a 7-day free trial for website purchases exists in 15 countries (per VPNAlert's deal check, Sept 28, 2026).
6. **Affiliate program:** Yes — **NordVPN Affiliate Program** (run in-house; also listed via networks such as CJ/Impact in affiliate directories). Published structure: **100% commission on a new 1-month plan, 40% on new 1-year/2-year plans, + 30% on renewals**, 30-day cookie, paid by PayPal/wire. Minimum-payout figures conflict between directories (one says none, another says ~$100) — confirm in the dashboard before counting on it. The renewal commission is the standout: it keeps paying David when a referred family renews.
7. **Honest weaknesses:** Renewal price roughly triples the intro monthly rate. 10-device cap can pinch a big household (router install is the workaround). No port forwarding. Tier naming (Basic/Plus/Complete/Prime/Ultra) varies by region and upsells hard at checkout. A 2019 incident breached one rented server in Finland — disclosed by NordVPN; no user data was in it (no logs), and the fleet has since gone RAM-only with repeated Deloitte audits of the no-logs policy.

## 2. ExpressVPN
1. **Why it's in the four:** Tied best unblocking — **14 of 14** in Gizmodo's streaming test; Top10VPN's Roku testing credits it with **18 Netflix regions**, HBO Max, Hulu, BBC iPlayer. MediaStreamer (its SmartDNS) is the most polished of the four.
2. **Speed evidence:** Gizmodo streaming table: **7.8% PC speed loss, 4.6% on Fire TV Stick**; in Gizmodo's dedicated fastest-VPN runs it averaged **11.3% download loss** across UK/US/Japan — a step behind NordVPN on nearby servers, but reviewers consistently praise its *long-distance* consistency. Protocol: **Lightway** (its own, open-source-audited design).
3. **Countries:** **105 countries, 3,000+ servers** (long-standing vendor figure — smallest server fleet of the four, widest polish per server).
4. **Devices + router:** **Best router story in the business, and it's not close.** New 2026 tiers: Basic 10 / Advanced 12 / Pro 14 simultaneous devices. It sells **Aircove**, a router with ExpressVPN built in, and offers custom firmware/app for Asus, Linksys and Netgear routers; the router + everything behind it counts as **one** connection. MediaStreamer covers Roku, Samsung/LG TVs and consoles that can't run apps. Top10VPN rates it **#1 for Roku (9.8/10)** and notes it's the only provider with dedicated software for *every* Roku workaround — directly relevant to our Roku-style TV ambitions.
5. **Best offer seen 2026-10-20:** A 2026 pricing reset made it far cheaper than its old "premium" reputation: Basic 2-year at **$2.49/mo for the first 28 months** (incl. 4 bonus months), Advanced $2.99, Pro $5.49 (per Grey's plan breakdown and VPNPro's Oct 2026 deal roundup). Renews at a higher annual rate. 30-day money-back guarantee; 7-day free trial on iOS/Android.
6. **Affiliate program:** Yes — **ExpressVPN affiliate program** (in-house, with dedicated affiliate managers). Pays a **flat CPA per sale: $13 (1-month) / $22 (6-month) / $36 (12-month)**, **no recurring/renewal commission**. Cookie length is *disputed by sources* — ROIAds says 90 days, other directories say 30 — must be confirmed on the official program page at signup. Minimum payout $100 (per ROIAds).
7. **Honest weaknesses:** Historically the most expensive of the four (the $2.49 intro is new — watch what it renews at). Smallest server count. No port forwarding. Owned by **Kape Technologies** — the same parent as CyberGhost and PIA; some privacy purists dislike the consolidation (its no-logs policy is independently audited, KPMG among others). Affiliate pays once and never again — worst long-term economics of the four for a shareable link.

## 3. Surfshark
1. **Why it's in the four:** **13 of 14** services unblocked in Gizmodo's test (Netflix, Disney+, Max and more per SafetyDetectives), Smart DNS included — and one feature nobody else here has (see #4).
2. **Speed evidence:** Gizmodo Oct 2026: **9.6% download loss, 5.9% upload loss** — third of the four, comfortably 4K-capable. Cybernews' 2026 table: 17% average loss, ranked #2 overall there. Other testers note distant-server upload speeds sag more than NordVPN's. Protocol: **WireGuard** (also OpenVPN/IKEv2).
3. **Countries:** **100 countries, 4,500+ servers** (current vendor/review figure; older reviews say 3,200+).
4. **Devices + router:** **UNLIMITED simultaneous devices** — the single best family feature in this showdown: every TV, phone, tablet and laptop in the house on one account, no counting. Router setup supported (manual config guides), Smart DNS for consoles/TVs, apps for Fire TV/Android TV/Apple TV.
5. **Best offer seen 2026-10-20:** Starter **$2.49/mo — $67.23 for 27 months** (2-year + 3 bonus months, up to ~85% off). A Black Friday campaign (Oct 12–Dec 1, per BestBackupReviews) upgrades buyers to Surfshark One at the Starter price. Beware the month-to-month rate: **$16.45/mo** — the deals are all in the long plans. 30-day money-back guarantee; 7-day trial via app stores.
6. **Affiliate program:** Yes — **Surfshark Affiliate Program**, available through Impact, Awin, CJ and TUNE: **40%+ revenue share on new sales**, with performance bonuses/custom rates for volume; 30-day cookie; $100 minimum payout; PayPal/wire. Whether renewals pay is *not clearly published* (sources describe bonuses rather than a standard renewal revshare) — confirm at signup.
7. **Honest weaknesses:** Based in the Netherlands (9 Eyes jurisdiction — mitigated by Deloitte-audited no-logs, but it's the fact pattern). Distant-server speeds trail NordVPN. Some locations are virtual servers. Features vary by platform. Renewal prices jump after the intro term. Unreliable in heavily restricted countries (China). **And the showdown-relevant one: Surfshark has been owned by Nord Security — NordVPN's parent — since 2022.** The networks run independently, but as a *failover* for NordVPN it shares a corporate roof.

## 4. CyberGhost
1. **Why it's in the four:** The beginner/streaming-discovery pick: **servers labeled by service** ("for Netflix US," "for BBC iPlayer") so a non-technical viewer never guesses. Gizmodo: **11 of 14** unblocked; VPNStreamer's review credits **16+ Netflix libraries** plus Disney+, Prime Video, Hulu, BBC iPlayer via those dedicated streaming servers. Smart DNS included.
2. **Speed evidence:** The slowest of the four in Gizmodo's Oct 2026 runs: **13.3% average download loss** (mostly on distant servers; nearby servers are fast, and StreamFix measured 877 Mbps local). Still far above the ~15 Mbps a 4K Netflix stream needs on any decent home connection. Protocol: **WireGuard** (also OpenVPN/IKEv2).
3. **Countries:** **100 countries**; server totals quoted 9,700–11,700 across recent reviews — among the largest fleets here.
4. **Devices + router:** Only **7 simultaneous devices** — the tightest cap of the four — though a router install covers the whole home through one slot. Apps for Fire TV/Android TV/routers; task-grouped server lists (streaming/gaming/torrenting).
5. **Best offer seen 2026-10-20:** Cheapest seat in the showdown: **$2.19/mo — $56.94 for 26 months** (Gizmodo); some outlets saw $1.99/mo ($51.74/26 mo). Renews ~$56.94/year (~$4.75/mo) — the *gentlest* renewal of the four. **45-day money-back guarantee on plans over a month — the longest test window in this report** (14 days on the monthly plan).
6. **Affiliate program:** Yes — **CyberGhost Affiliate Program** via HasOffers/TUNE (managed by Kape; signup via affiliates.kape.com): **up to 100% commission on first sales** (per-plan rates aren't fully public — set with an affiliate manager), **45-day cookie** (longest here), $100 minimum payout per directories, PayPal/wire. No recurring commission.
7. **Honest weaknesses:** Weakest speed and joint-weakest unblocking of the four (Netflix Japan and JioHotstar were among Gizmodo's misses). 7-device cap with a device-registration system some users find awkward. **No obfuscation at all** — doesn't work in China or on networks that block VPNs. Gizmodo's testing caught its kill switch leaking on PC restart. Owned by Kape Technologies (same parent as ExpressVPN).

## 🏆 Judged ranking for OUR use case

**1. NordVPN — PRIMARY.** It wins the things the TV station runs on: perfect 14/14 unblocking, the fastest measured speeds (and the best Fire TV Stick number — our actual hardware class), the widest country menu for hopping through our 26-country library, SmartDNS for dumb TVs, and the only affiliate deal that pays on *renewals* (40% new + 30% recurring) — the difference between a code that pays once and a code that pays David for years.

**2. ExpressVPN — BACKUP.** Chosen as the failover precisely because it shares *nothing* with the primary: different owner (Kape, not Nord Security), different network, different protocol (Lightway, not WireGuard-family). If NordVPN has an outage, a blocked-IP wave, or a corporate problem, ExpressVPN is a genuinely independent second engine — and it ties NordVPN at 14/14 unblocking. It also has the best router/Roku story (Aircove + its own router firmware + MediaStreamer), which plugs straight into Koda Kai TV's Roku-style ambitions and covers the TVs and consoles that can't run apps. Its costs: watch the renewal price, and its affiliate pays a flat one-time bounty — so the TV page leads with NordVPN's link and keeps ExpressVPN as the "if the switch ever fails" second code.

**3. Surfshark — the family-value alternate.** If the binding constraint turns out to be device count in a house full of screens, Surfshark's unlimited devices at $2.49/mo is the rational pick, and the judges should say so on the podcast. It loses the backup slot only on independence: sharing NordVPN's parent makes it a weaker *insurance policy*, not a weaker VPN.

**4. CyberGhost — the beginner/budget alternate.** Cheapest, friendliest labeled streaming servers, gentlest renewal, and the 45-day refund makes it the easiest to test. But 11/14 unblocking, the slowest speeds, 7 devices and no obfuscation keep it fourth *for this use case*.

## 🧪 Hands-on tests to run BEFORE crowning (nothing bought yet)
Free trials and money-back windows make a real race possible without committing: NordVPN's 7-day website trial (15 countries) / 30-day guarantee, ExpressVPN's 7-day mobile trial / 30-day guarantee, Surfshark's 7-day app-store trial / 30-day guarantee, CyberGhost's free desktop trial + **45-day** guarantee. **Any purchase needs David's exact yes first** — and if we test with paid plans, buy *one at a time*, test inside its window, and calendar the refund deadline on day 1.

1. **Our-library unblock matrix:** from the Clearwater connection, test BBC iPlayer, Netflix US/UK/JP, Disney+, David's actual subscriptions, plus free-to-air targets from our own TV library (Pluto/Tubi US, NHK, DW) — the exact list the TV page will promise.
2. **Evening-peak speed runs:** Ookla baseline vs connected (US/UK/Japan), 3 evenings in a row — Gizmodo's method — plus a Fire TV Stick run, because the stick is where numbers diverge.
3. **Router test:** install on the home router (Aircove trial for ExpressVPN), confirm the Roku/smart TVs are covered, and time a country switch end-to-end.
4. **Failover drill:** kill the primary mid-stream, flip to the backup, time how long until the picture is back. This is the user's stated requirement — test it like we mean it.
5. **Whole-house stress:** every screen in the home streaming at once; watch Surfshark's unlimited claim vs the 10/14/7 caps.
6. **Leak checks:** DNS/WebRTC/IP leak tests and a kill-switch pull-the-plug test on each (CyberGhost has something to prove here).
7. **Support test:** one real live-chat question per provider ("which server for BBC iPlayer tonight?") — graded on answer quality; this becomes a podcast segment.
8. **Refund rehearsal (finalists only):** actually exercise one money-back refund to prove the window is real before recommending it to the audience.
