# 🎬 KODA KAI TV — AD & COMMERCIAL PRODUCTION PIPELINE v0.1
David's commission, 2026-10-20: the TV station is also the start of our TV ad
production — commercials made from successful scripts and podcasts, aired as real
television on our own category stations.

## The pipeline (script → screen)
1. **Prove it in audio first.** A topic earns a podcast (panel, research, verdict —
   e.g. the Four-VPN Showdown). If the script doesn't land as audio, it never
   becomes a commercial. The podcast IS the audition.
2. **Cut the commercial script.** From the winning material: 30s hero + 15s cut +
   6s bumper, all carrying the code 3 ways (spoken, on-screen text, QR/short link) —
   the Marquee doctrine, moved to TV.
3. **Storyboard + produce.** Scenes from the storyboard: our avatar/voice cast
   (established voices only), generated visuals, caption-burned from the start
   (Language Sublayer renders the spot per region — tone + Courtesy Gate applied,
   so one commercial becomes a respectful spot in every country's register).
4. **Air on our own stations first.** House rotation across the category stations
   (below) — the network is the first network. Proof-of-airing logged like
   proof-of-engagement: timestamp, station, spot version.
5. **Then out the door.** The same package feeds Marquee/Blip billboards and
   social — one production, every surface. Client version of this pipeline is
   a KrysAI/Marquee service later; house ads prove it first.

## First production in queue
**The VPN Showdown spot** — gated in order: research lands → panel podcast picks
two winners → David signs up on his exact word (codes exist only then) →
commercial script names only real codes → produce → air on World News + Adventure
stations + TV page showdown slot. No code is ever advertised before it exists.

## Rules (standing)
- Every spot carries captions below/within frame by design, never covering faces.
- Courtesy Gate applies to ads in every language, same as programs.
- Claims in a commercial must survive the same grading as the podcast research —
  if the panel couldn't say it, the ad can't either.
- Nothing airs publicly beyond our own pages without David's per-item word.
